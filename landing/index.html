Questions?

Do I need to know Chinese already?

ChatMandarin supports HSK levels 1-6. Complete beginners can start with HSK 1 conversations — we'll guide you word by word.

What does ChatMandarin focus on?

ChatMandarin focuses on spoken Mandarin practice through AI conversations, speech feedback, shadowing, dubbing, and HSKK-style exercises.

Will the AI correct my tones?

ChatMandarin analyzes spoken responses and provides pronunciation and fluency feedback. AI feedback can be imperfect, so use it as learning guidance rather than an official assessment.

Is this useful if I'm not taking HSK?

Absolutely. HSK levels map to vocabulary difficulty. If you want "I can order food and have basic chats" level, that's roughly HSK 3.

Is ChatMandarin available now?

Yes. You can create a free account and begin practicing at app.chatmandarin.cc.

Is my data safe?

Voice input is processed to provide speech features. Please review our Privacy Policy for details about data handling and third-party service providers.
